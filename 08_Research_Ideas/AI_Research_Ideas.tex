\section*{From classroom to research}
Every topic in this course is an active research area. Each idea below states the \textbf{gap}, a concrete \textbf{method}, the \textbf{data and tools} needed, the \textbf{expected outcome} and suitable \textbf{publication venues}. Levels indicate scope: \colorbox{teal!15}{\textcolor{teal}{\textbf{UG Project}}} (one semester), \colorbox{navyL!15}{\textcolor{navyL}{\textbf{M.Tech / Minor Thesis}}} (one year) and \colorbox{crimson!15}{\textcolor{crimson}{\textbf{Ph.D. Direction}}} (multi-year).
\begin{keypoint}[How to turn an idea into a paper]
(1) Read 15--20 recent papers and write a one-page gap statement. (2) Reproduce one baseline. (3) Propose one clear change and test it on a public dataset with proper metrics and statistics. (4) Report negative results honestly. (5) Release code and data on GitHub. (6) Choose the venue whose recent papers your work cites most.
\end{keypoint}
\begin{keypoint}[Research ethics]
Obtain consent and institutional approval before collecting data from people; anonymise records; follow dataset licences; never fabricate or selectively report results.
\end{keypoint}

\begin{tcolorbox}[enhanced,breakable,colback=white,colframe=navyL,arc=2mm,boxrule=0.9pt,
 title={\textbf{RI.1\quad Measuring rationality of LLM-based agents with PEAS-grounded benchmarks}},fonttitle=\color{white},colbacktitle=navyL]
\colorbox{navyL!15}{\scriptsize\bfseries\color{navyL} M.TECH / MINOR THESIS}\par\smallskip
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{>{\leavevmode\bfseries\color{navy}}p{3.0cm}X}
Research gap & LLM agents are evaluated on task success only; there is no standard way to check whether their behaviour is rational for a declared performance measure.\\
Method & Define PEAS specifications for 10 tool-using tasks, log agent percept--action traces, score expected-utility regret against an oracle planner, and compare prompting strategies.\\
Data \& tools & Open-source LLMs, LangGraph / simple tool APIs, Python logging\\
Outcome & A benchmark and a 'rationality score' for agentic AI systems.\\
Target venues & IEEE Access; Expert Systems with Applications; AAMAS workshops\\
\end{tabularx}
\end{tcolorbox}
\begin{tcolorbox}[enhanced,breakable,colback=white,colframe=teal,arc=2mm,boxrule=0.9pt,
 title={\textbf{RI.2\quad Environment-property classifier for automatic agent-architecture selection}},fonttitle=\color{white},colbacktitle=teal]
\colorbox{teal!15}{\scriptsize\bfseries\color{teal} UG PROJECT}\par\smallskip
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{>{\leavevmode\bfseries\color{navy}}p{3.0cm}X}
Research gap & Students and engineers pick agent architectures by intuition; there is no tool that maps a problem description to environment properties.\\
Method & Build a labelled set of 300 task descriptions, fine-tune a small text classifier for the seven properties, and recommend an architecture with rules.\\
Data \& tools & Hugging Face transformers, scikit-learn, Streamlit\\
Outcome & A web tool that outputs PEAS, properties and a recommended agent type.\\
Target venues & Student conferences; IEEE ICCCNT; Springer LNNS\\
\end{tabularx}
\end{tcolorbox}

\unitbanner{II}{Problem Solving by Search}{4 Research Ideas \textbullet\ CO204.2}
\begin{tcolorbox}[enhanced,breakable,colback=white,colframe=crimson,arc=2mm,boxrule=0.9pt,
 title={\textbf{RII.1\quad Learned heuristics for A*: neural guidance with admissibility guarantees}},fonttitle=\color{white},colbacktitle=crimson]
\colorbox{crimson!15}{\scriptsize\bfseries\color{crimson} PH.D. DIRECTION}\par\smallskip
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{>{\leavevmode\bfseries\color{navy}}p{3.0cm}X}
Research gap & Neural heuristics speed up search but can over-estimate, losing optimality.\\
Method & Train a GNN to predict cost-to-go, then clip with an admissible bound or use bounded-suboptimal search (weighted A*, focal search) with proven error bounds.\\
Data \& tools & PyTorch Geometric, 8/15-puzzle and grid-map benchmarks\\
Outcome & Faster search with guaranteed bounded sub-optimality.\\
Target venues & AAAI; IJCAI; SoCS (Symposium on Combinatorial Search)\\
\end{tabularx}
\end{tcolorbox}
\begin{tcolorbox}[enhanced,breakable,colback=white,colframe=navyL,arc=2mm,boxrule=0.9pt,
 title={\textbf{RII.2\quad Exam and lab timetabling for Indian universities as a hybrid CSP + metaheuristic}},fonttitle=\color{white},colbacktitle=navyL]
\colorbox{navyL!15}{\scriptsize\bfseries\color{navyL} M.TECH / MINOR THESIS}\par\smallskip
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{>{\leavevmode\bfseries\color{navy}}p{3.0cm}X}
Research gap & Manual timetabling causes clashes and poor room use; generic solvers ignore local rules such as practical batches.\\
Method & Model batches, rooms and faculty as a CSP; solve hard constraints with CP-SAT, optimise soft constraints with simulated annealing; compare with manual timetables.\\
Data \& tools & Google OR-Tools CP-SAT, Python, real (anonymised) department data\\
Outcome & A deployable timetable generator with measurable clash and utilisation gains.\\
Target venues & Journal of Scheduling; Computers \& Operations Research\\
\end{tabularx}
\end{tcolorbox}
